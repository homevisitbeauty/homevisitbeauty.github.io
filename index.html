Questions to ask before booking a home waxing visit in Bangkok

Home Visit Beauty, a mobile beauty service in Bangkok, wrote this checklist for waxing visits at a hotel or condo. The questions suit any home waxing provider.

What can you ask on WhatsApp before you book?

You can ask which wax is used, whether applicators are single-use and how often the practitioner has done your treatment.

Clear answers without hesitation are a good sign. Also ask how the practitioner will get into the building when you are in a hotel.

What changes in a hotel or a condo?

Hotels usually ask visitors for ID at the desk, and condos are often stricter.

Condos usually want photo ID, and some buildings have quiet hours. Home Visit Beauty asks for the building name at booking and says what the practitioner needs to bring. Sometimes a name is left at the desk.

What does Home Visit Beauty ask of you?

Home Visit Beauty asks for roughly 2 square metres of free floor and a nearby power outlet.

The practitioner also expects skin that is clean and dry, free of lotion, oil and fake tan. Wax grips only when hair is at least 5 mm long. Payment is cash in Thai baht.

What should arrive with the practitioner?

Everything the treatment needs should arrive with the practitioner and leave with them.

  • Applicators that are never dipped twice.
  • Tools cleaned between clients.
  • A cover or fresh towels between you and your own linen.
  • Wax that stays sealed until the appointment.

Which signs mean you should decline the visit?

Six signs mean you should decline the visit.

  • There is no booking confirmation or written record.
  • Cash is demanded before any service starts.
  • You are pushed to add services or extend the time you booked.
  • No tools or products are visible.
  • Wax pots or applicators are reused.
  • The provider avoids questions about hygiene.

How does Home Visit Beauty handle hygiene and wax choice?

Home Visit Beauty opens a fresh pot of wax for each booking, and clients choose RICA, Aloevera or Honey.

  • Applicators are single-use.
  • Tools arrive in a sealed pouch that is opened at the appointment.
  • Every used item leaves with the practitioner.
  • The price is quoted on WhatsApp before the visit.

Where does Home Visit Beauty travel?
